# Demo script: five minutes

Tidewell Lines, the default scenario: a fictional container carrier in Bremen, read from three challenger
reports. Every figure below is the engine's; the guide's talk track quotes the same. The German script is
in DEMO_SCRIPT.de.md.

## Before you start

- Open the live site in a fresh tab, full screen, at 1920 × 1080 or 1280 × 720. *How it works* opens by
  itself on its first chapter; `?guide=present` opens it straight on the talk track, which ends with
  **Start**.
- Close the guide with × or its **Start**. On the page, **Start** begins the run and starts Play, which
  walks the steps and stops wherever you act: open an idea or a weight and it waits for you. Space pauses
  while you talk; the right arrow or a clicker moves on.
- No key is needed. The simulation calls no model and reaches no other site.

## If something goes wrong

- **A wrong click.** Keep undoes a merge or a park. **Back** steps back, and **Reset the weights** brings
  the default weights back.
- **Lost on the way.** `?scenario=s1&step=4` opens Score; `step=6` opens the report with the recorded
  shortlist.
- **A PDF you can't search.** Choose *Save as PDF* as the printer. *Microsoft Print to PDF* turns the text
  into shapes and makes the file several times larger.
- **No page numbers.** Print from Chrome or Edge. Other browsers may print the footer without them.
- **A small projector.** The page fits 1280 × 720. Lower the resolution rather than zoom in.

## Questions that will come

**Is this real?** Tidewell Lines and its own figures are invented, and its three challenger reports are
stand-ins written for the scenario, which say so. What they say about the world is real and dated: from
2027 carriers surrender emission allowances for all the emissions the EU system counts, FuelEU Maritime
has applied since 1 January 2025, and 60 % of Asia–Europe capacity runs through Suez again. DATA_SOURCES.md lists every
source with the day it was read.

**Where do the figures come from?** From code. The Ideator rates each idea from 1 to 5 on seven criteria,
each with its reason; the engine weighs the ratings, ranks the ideas, checks the ranking nine times and
works out every key figure. A fact audit refuses any other number in the agents' words.

**What does a run cost?** A replay costs nothing. A live run of these three reports is estimated at about
€0.01 on the Balanced setting and about €0.11 on Thorough. With **Run it live**, the agents read your
documents on your own Claude key, up to a cap you set.

**Does it build the prototype?** No: it proposes and ranks. The build brief turns the chosen idea into
the instructions and the first message for a new Claude project, which builds it the way this tool was
built.
